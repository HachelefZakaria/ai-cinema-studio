# Code of Conduct

AI Cinema Studio is intended to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive
community for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not on people.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discriminatory language, and unwanted contact.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private
  information.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when conduct violates these expectations. For concerns, contact
the maintainers through the repository's available private reporting channel.
